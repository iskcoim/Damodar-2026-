Disappearance Day Ceremony

Remembering our Founder-Acharya

His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Srila Prabhupada left this world on 14 November 1977 in Vrindavan, surrounded by his disciples and the sound of the holy names. In the eleven years since he had founded ISKCON he had carried Lord Krishna's teachings around the world.

His Tirobhava Mahotsava is the ceremony of his disappearance day. Devotees gather to remember him and to speak and write about what he has given them. Every temple of ISKCON, including ours, exists because of him.

At Sri Jagannath Mandir, Coimbatore. Everyone is welcome.

1896 to 1977

His journey

  1. 1896

    Born in Kolkata as Abhay Charan De, and raised by his father in devotion to Lord Krishna

  2. 1922

    Meets his guru, Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati, who asks him to preach in English

  3. 1965

    Sails to America aboard the Jaladuta, at the age of sixty-nine

  4. 1966

    Founds the International Society for Krishna Consciousness in New York City

  5. 1967

    With Lord Jagannath in Golden Gate Park, San Francisco

  6. 1974

    On a morning walk with his disciples in Germany, during his travels around the world

  7. 1977

    Departs in Vrindavan with his disciples at his side, leaving a worldwide family of temples and devotees
