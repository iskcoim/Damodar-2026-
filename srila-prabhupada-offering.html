Your offering to Srila Prabhupada

A few heartfelt words, in your own way and in your own language.

Why offer your words?

On this day disciples and followers offer their gratitude to Srila Prabhupada. You don't need to be a writer, or to have met him. If his books, his temples, the holy name or his devotees have touched your life, you have something to offer.

  • It is an act of devotionRemembering him and glorifying him is itself a service to him.
  • It deepens your gratitudePutting his gifts into words helps you see how much he has given you.
  • It inspires othersYour story may help another devotee or seeker come closer to Krishna.
