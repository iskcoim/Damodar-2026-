Tirobhava Mahotsava · 13 November 2026

Let us make this festival grand

For our Founder-Acharya, who gave us everything.

This festival is ours to offer

Srila Prabhupada crossed the ocean at sixty-nine with almost nothing, so that Krishna could reach us. Every temple, every book and every plate of prasadam we receive today comes from his sacrifice.

His Tirobhava Mahotsava is our chance to show our gratitude. It is we devotees who come together and make this day grand: the flowers, the feast, the books. Whatever you offer, small or large, is a way of honouring him.
